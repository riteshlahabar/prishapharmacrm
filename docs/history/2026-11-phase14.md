# Phase 14 — Products (2026-11-24)

A **Products** master (name, box qty, cost), `GET /api/v1/products` for both
apps, and optional product lines on a visit and a call log — repeatable rows
with a **+** below the notes, from one shared `ProductLinesField`.

Stock is the admin's alone: recording a product on a visit moves nothing.

A line snapshots `unit_cost`, so a price change never rewrites a visit that
already happened (`ProductService::priceLines`, which the siblings call
through the contract). A product used on a line cannot be deleted —
deactivate it instead.

Also fixed: the MR home goldens embedded the live date and would have failed
every morning; it comes from `todayProvider` now.
