# Phase 11 — reports        2026-11-22

Six management reports + CSV. Module: `docs/modules/reports.md`.

## Why
The panel had four **activity logs** (attendance, visits, calls, messages):
one row per event, plus a dashboard. Missing was a row per *person* — you
could filter the log to one rep but not compare nine — and nothing left the
panel as a file.

## The six
- **mr** — days present, hours, visits, persons seen, visits per *active*
  day, coverage % of the rep's assigned contacts, geo-tagged %.
- **telecaller** — calls, conversion %, status mix, call-backs, overdue,
  messages passed to an MR.
- **coverage** — active contacts untouched for 14/30/60/90 days,
  never-touched first. The only actionable one.
- **region** — reps, telecallers, contacts, visits, calls, touches/contact.
- **register** — the monthly P/O/A grid. A day with no row is absent; the MR
  app writes one on check-in, so a missing day is real.
- **follow-ups** — call-backs promised vs honoured, and honoured *on time*:
  `honoured` alone scores one made three weeks late as a success.

## Architecture
Reports owns no table and runs no query. Each source module gained a rollup
in its repository and a clamped `…ForUser` method on its service, which is
all Reports may call. Only clamped forms exist — the Phase 8 attendance bug
was a report reaching for the unclamped one.

Territory clamps reps, regions, coverage and the register; the telecaller
report and follow-ups clamp by *caller*, so a telecaller sees one row. An
empty territory means nobody, never everybody.

## Export
Streamed CSV, no new dependency. `ReportDefinition` drives the head, the sort
links and the file, so the download cannot miss a column. UTF-8 BOM for
Excel, `no-store`, and `=+-@` quoted so a contact named `=cmd|…` cannot
execute on open.

## Deliberately out
Excel/PDF (needs PhpSpreadsheet or dompdf), scheduled e-mail (no queue or
mail), period comparison, caching (measure first). Filter dropdowns *are*
scoped here, unlike the older module reports.
